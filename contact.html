Before you enquire

A few useful answers.

A little more clarity before we start planning together.

How long until we receive our photographs?

Sneak peeks typically arrive within 48 hours of the shoot. Full edited galleries are delivered within 2–3 weeks for portraits and 4–6 weeks for weddings and events — confirmed with your booking so you always know what to expect.

Do we get the raw, unedited files?

We don’t release raw files — editing is half of the craft, and raws don’t represent the finished work. You receive every worthwhile moment, fully edited, in print-ready and web-ready versions.

What deposit do you need to hold our date?

A 25% deposit confirms your booking; the balance is due after delivery. Until the deposit is paid, dates stay open — so if you love a date, don’t wait too long.

What happens if we need to reschedule?

Life happens, especially with families. Your deposit moves once, free of charge, to any mutually available date. For cancellations, the deposit terms are set out clearly before you pay anything.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

We photograph and film portraits, families, weddings, events, brands, documentary work and creative projects. If yours sits between categories, tell us about it and we’ll work out the right approach together.

How far ahead should I get in touch?

Availability depends on the date and type of project, and popular dates can fill quickly. Send your preferred date and location and we’ll let you know what’s possible. If your plans are soon, it’s still worth asking.

How is pricing worked out?

Every project is different. We’ll shape a quote around the coverage, deliverables, location and timing you need, and talk it through with you before you decide.

Can you work outside Liverpool?

We’re based in Fazakerley, Liverpool. Include your shoot location in the enquiry and we’ll confirm availability, travel arrangements and any related costs before booking.

What if I’m not sure exactly what I need?

That’s completely fine. Share what you’re planning, what matters most and any early ideas you have. We can help you find a format and approach that feels right.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

We’ll review the details and get back to you to discuss your plans, availability and next steps. Your enquiry is a conversation starter, not a confirmed booking.
