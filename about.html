Who we are · Neef Studios

We photograph the parts you forget to notice.

Neef Studios is a photography and film studio in Fazakerley, working across Liverpool and the North West. We shoot families, weddings, events, brands and documentary projects, and we work the same way on a ninety-minute portrait session as we do on a twelve-hour wedding: pay attention, keep the direction light, and give the ordinary details the same care as the set pieces.

Our approach

The moments in between make the story.

Most people tell us they hate having their photograph taken. That is usually the last thing they say before they forget the camera is there, which is the point. We do not arrive with a shot list to work through. We arrive, we talk, we let the session settle, and then we photograph what actually happens — the child who will only sit on one chair, the grandmother who does not know she is being photographed, the third attempt at a family portrait that turns out to be the one.

On a wedding day that means covering the milestones properly and then getting out of the way. On a brand shoot it means photographing the work rather than a mood board version of it: the roastery floor, the delivery van, the person who has done the job for nineteen years. The craft is in noticing, and in being quick enough when it matters.

Our standard

Years from now, our work should take you back to how it actually felt.

That is the test we hold every photograph and every frame of film against before it leaves the studio.

From your first idea to the final frame

Thoughtful at every step

01 / DISCOVER

Listen & understand

A conversation, not a questionnaire. We find out who matters, what the day or the brand actually needs, and where the boundaries are — including the parts you would rather we did not photograph.

02 / MAKE

Plan lightly, then work

We agree a simple structure, confirm timings and light, then treat the plan as a safety net rather than a script. Most of the photographs you will love were not on it.

03 / DELIVER

Edit & hand over

Every image is edited individually. You get a private gallery, print-ready files, and the turnaround we promised at booking — sneak peeks within 48 hours.
