A clear note on your information

Privacy

This notice explains what happens when you browse the Neef Studios website or send an enquiry. Last updated: 3 October 2026.

01 / Who is responsible

Your enquiry stays personal.

Neef Studios is responsible for the information you choose to share through this website. For questions or a request about your information, email Neefstudios.uk@gmail.com.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information in an enquiry unless it is necessary for us to respond to your request.

02 / What we receive

Only what you choose to share.

If you contact the studio, the enquiry form may ask for your name, contact details, the kind of photography or film you are interested in, dates, location and a project message. The exact fields depend on the form configuration.

The site also uses third-party services for the enquiry form, externally hosted images and, on the contact page, the embedded map. Those providers receive technical information from your browser when their content loads. Their own privacy notices apply to that processing.

03 / Why and how long

Used to respond and do business.

For an enquiry, Neef Studios uses your details to reply and take steps you request before a possible booking. If you book, details are used to provide the agreed service and meet legal record-keeping duties. Limited administration may rely on the studio’s legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and running its business. The studio does not sell enquiry details and keeps them only as long as needed for those purposes and applicable record-keeping duties.

Enquiries sent through the booking form are handled directly by the studio and sent to the studio email as part of a standard contact request. Please review the details before sending.

04 / Your choices

Ask, correct or request removal.

You can contact the studio to ask about information you have submitted, request a correction or ask for deletion where applicable. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office. Learn more at ico.org.uk.

If you email or send a booking enquiry, the information included in the message is used only for the purpose of responding to your request. Please keep any sensitive personal information to what is necessary for the enquiry.
