Who we are

We are paid to notice things.

Neef Studios is a photography and film studio in Fazakerley, Liverpool. We photograph families at home on a Saturday morning, weddings from the first cup of tea to the last dance, brands that want their actual work shown properly, and documentary projects that take months rather than hours.

Our approach is the same every time: ask good questions, plan enough to relax, then photograph what really happens. The results are the kind of images that still mean something in twenty years, when the details you think you will never forget have started to blur.
